How to read this

Lines in quote marks are the document's own words, with the page. In plain wordslines are ours, and when the two differ, the document is right.

Where a state's documents are in Hindi or another Indian language, we don't quote them here: you see our plain English with the page, and a link to open the official document.

"Not in the document" means we read the whole document and it doesn't say. The rule may sit in a separate notice for that round, so look there before you decide.

The rounds are lined up by number. MCC's data-sharing rules fix the order at Round 3 and the Stray Round. For the exact dates, see each year's schedule.
